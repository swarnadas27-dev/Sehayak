Slow Learner Identification & Remedial Support System

SEHAYAK is an early identification and remedial learning platform designed to support every student at their own pace. The system evaluates assessment performance, attendance health, and examination answer sheets to identify learning gaps early—generating strengths-based remedial pathways, multisensory CBSE practice, and seamless collaboration between teachers, parents, students, and school leadership.

Algorithm

Curriculum

Smart Front-Sheet Grading

Digitally evaluates CBSE assessments with structured marks and feedback.

Intervention

Track student interventions, milestones and learning outcomes.

  • Milestone-based progress tracking.
  • Post-intervention assessment.

Ethical & Supportive by Design

SEHAYAK supports educational screening, not clinical diagnosis. Its insights help educators provide inclusive, personalized learning support.
