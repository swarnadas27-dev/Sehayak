SEHAYAK • सहायक Slow Learner Identification & Remedial Support System

SEHAYAK is an early identification and remedial learning platform designed to support every student at their own pace. The system evaluates assessment performance, attendance health, and examination answer sheets to identify learning gaps early—generating strengths-based remedial pathways, multisensory CBSE practice, and seamless collaboration between teachers, parents, students, and school leadership.

Unique Innovative Features of SEHAYAK

Algorithm

Dual-Engine Explainable AI

Scikit-Learn Random Forest model paired with transparent pedagogical heuristic safeguards. Features SHAP-style local factor contribution breakdowns.

Curriculum

CBSE Front-Sheet Grading

Digital front-cover rubric with 1-to-15 question sequence (50 Marks), marks obtained tabulation, and qualitative remarks on specific student weaknesses and growth.

Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA/AAA Dyslexia Suite

Specialized Lexend and OpenDyslexic typography, cursor-tracking Reading Ruler focus bar, Irlen color overlays, and bilingual Web Speech read-aloud.

Intervention

Closed-Loop RTI Progress Tracker

Transforms static recommendations into an active Response to Intervention cycle with milestone checkboxes, live progress bars, and post-test efficacy verification.

Ethical Philosophy: Flags are for Support, Never for Stigmatization

SEHAYAK is designed strictly as an educational screening and pedagogical capacity building platform. It does not provide clinical diagnoses of learning disabilities. All insights exist to guide educators and parents in tailoring multisensory instruction.
